# 🌅 Sunrise Hotel Website

A **modern website** for **Sunrise Hotel**, built with **HTML5**, **CSS3**, and **JavaScript**.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Modern UI/UX** – Clean, professional design with smooth animations  
- **Multiple Pages** – Complete website with all essential sections  
- **Interactive Elements** – Forms, navigation, and user interactions  
- **Accessibility Focused** – Semantic HTML and proper alt attributes  

---

## 🚀 Pages Overview

### 🏠 Home Page
- Welcoming hero banner with hotel slogan  
- Featured rooms & suites showcase  
- Special offers & promotions  
- Newsletter subscription  

### 🛏️ Rooms & Facilities
- Detailed room descriptions (Deluxe, Suite, Family, Standard)  
- Room images and pricing  
- “Book Now” functionality  
- Hotel facilities (pool, gym, restaurant, spa, conference hall)  

### 🍽️ Dining & Services
- Restaurant, café, and bar highlights  
- Menu items with images and prices  
- Additional services (airport shuttle, room service, event hosting)  

### 💬 Testimonials
- Customer reviews with names and dates  
- Testimonial videos and audio  
- Interactive feedback checkboxes  

### 📞 Contact Us
- Hotel location and contact details  
- Inquiry form with validation  
- Social media links
